## Capstone Story Outline

## 1. The Problem
Hotel rooms are perishable; forecasting drives staffing, purchasing, and seasonal openings.

## 2. The Data Journey
Analyzing 185 Swiss communes (2013–2026). Handling missing data codes, merging Moutier's canton change, and managing the 2020 pandemic structural break.

## 3. The Solution
Training models (Seasonal Naive, SARIMA, ML) on 2013–2019 data, stress-testing on 2020–2022, and testing on 2023+ data. Comparing city vs. alpine resort segments.

## 4. The Impact
Translating forecast errors into room nights and occupancy points to help managers plan maintenance, marketing, and capacity.
